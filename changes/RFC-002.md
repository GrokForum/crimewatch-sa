## How success is proven

1. `curl --cacert ci/certs/saps-chain.pem` downloads the official workbook; the same `curl` without `-k` and without the bundle fails TLS verification.
2. The downloaded file matches the reviewed sha256 and size in `data/manifest.json`.
3. `python -m pytest -q` passes, including the new failing-fixture, CLI, dispute, merge, province, pin and POPIA tests.
4. `python pipeline/dq_check.py` exits 0 and prints both known DISPUTED lines.
5. `python pipeline/popia_gate.py` exits 0 on the committed data.
6. review_kit is clean on the new head, or every finding is explained.
7. Commits use the publisher identity only. No staff or bot names and no absolute machine paths in committed files.
