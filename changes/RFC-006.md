## 1. Problem and why it matters

Official SAPS recorded-crime statistics remain quarterly. The current public series on CrimeWatch is Q2 2026/27 (July–September 2026), already cited on the live method page (https://crimewatch.grokforum.site/method/, edition of 20 November 2026) from:

- https://www.saps.gov.za/services/downloads/2026/2026-2027_-_2nd_Quarter_WEB.pdf
- https://www.saps.gov.za/services/downloads/2026/2026-2027_-_2nd_Quarter_WEB.xlsx

There is no official daily national crime table in the sources checked on 25 Nov 2026. South Africans still see incidents announced most days in SAPS media statements, and they look for one place that gathers those announcements by official category.

This RFC does not invent such a table. It proposes a **labelled daily register of public SAPS media statements**, each item mapped onto one of the 44 official Q2 `Crime_Category` names or marked **Unmapped**, with social posts and news RSS used only as cite-and-link leads, and court material taken only from original court or judiciary sources — never from SAFLII. Court ingest is **not in v1**.

## 2. Goal

Add a daily, labelled, heavily redacted register to CrimeWatch so a reader can see, by official category and by date, what SAPS chose to announce, with a link to the original statement.

The first surface is the **private draft surface**. The public page stays held until a later public-page GO. Any public build of this register stays `noindex` until a named GO that says otherwise. A `daily_register=on` build, or any public `/daily/` build, is refused unless answers (a) and (b) and a public-page GO are all recorded (§11). The public-page GO is not recorded yet, so that check currently refuses.

Success is proven when all of the following hold in DEV, and later in TEST, before any PROD GO:

1. New statement items are stored as statement records, not as recorded-crime counts.
2. Each item has exactly one of the 44 official category names, or `Unmapped`. No 45th official category is invented.
3. Quarterly CSVs, heat-map layers and Proven national totals are bit-identical to the pre-change SHA after a daily run.
4. The site gate refuses any page that places daily statement-item counts on, or adjacent as if additive to, quarterly Proven totals or heat-map layers.
5. The POPIA gate fails closed on private names, ages, contact details, identity numbers, number plates, precise victim or street/farm locations, photographs of private people, and on a unique combination of date + place + role/age (especially of minors), even when SAPS published that combination (§8).
6. Every public row carries the default claim framing “SAPS claim”, one CrimeWatch fact label from the closed set Proven / Established / Alleged / Disputed / Not established, and the `daily_map.py` mapping shown as CrimeWatch’s interpretation (§6, §9).
7. The exact owner disclaimer appears, worded exactly: “We are not responsible for anything published by anyone else, including personal information.” The only contact published on this product is `privacy@grokforum.site`.
8. The page states, next to the figures, that this is a register of announcements, not a daily SAPS census, and that official national totals remain quarterly.
9. The job is a step of the same 05:55 CrimeWatch daily sweep that RFC-005 uses, joined on the canonical SAPS statement URL, with no unsynced double publication (§5). Its usage is measured in DEV before any standing schedule. This RFC does not invent a measured cost for the new work.
10. Day summaries contain no unsourced incident detail and no detail from a POPIA-excluded item.

## 4. Data model

Suggested store (DEV paths; names may be adjusted in the repo RFC, not the quarterly tree):

- `data/daily/statements.jsonl` — one object per official statement, keyed by the **canonical SAPS statement URL** (the join key with RFC-005).
- `data/daily/items.jsonl` — one object per mapped item (a statement may yield more than one item when it clearly names more than one official category).
- Generated pages only, no writes to `data/saps_q2_2026_27_long.csv` or the layer CSVs, and no silent rewrite of `data/sweeps.json`. The 05:55 pass may prepare a join-keyed RFC-005 day object in the same run, but it does not skip RFC-005’s review-before-publish: a new or updated sweeps edition is reviewed like any other content change before it is published. There is no auto-publish of `data/sweeps.json` from the morning pass.

### 4.1 Join key with RFC-005

The one join key is the **canonical SAPS statement URL** (scheme and host normalised, tracking query stripped, the official `saps.gov.za` newsroom URL preferred over a gov.za or social copy).

- In this register, `source_url` holds that URL. `statement_id` may be a hash of it, but other products join on the URL itself, not on the hash.
- An RFC-005 `sweeps.json` item that cites the same statement must include that same URL in `sources`.
- The 05:55 job may prepare or update both sides from one pass, or leave the RFC-005 day object without a duplicate, unsynced telling of the same statement. Preparing that object does not skip RFC-005’s review-before-publish.
- No unsynced double publication: the two public paths (`/daily/` if later GOed, and `/daily-sweeps/`) must not show conflicting dates, claims, or category wording for the same URL, and one path must not publish a statement the other still treats as excluded.

### 4.2 Statement record (minimum fields)

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `source_url` | Canonical SAPS statement URL (join key) |
| `statement_id` | Hash of `source_url`, for this file only |
| `source_host` | `saps.gov.za` or `gov.za` |
| `statement_date` | Date as published, Africa/Johannesburg |
| `fetched_at` | Fetch timestamp, Africa/Johannesburg, with zone |
| `title_redacted` | Title after the POPIA gate |
| `claim_redacted` | Short labelled claim after the POPIA gate; not the full body unless SAPS has given written reuse permission (see §7 and §12) |
| `claim_framing` | Always `SAPS claim` for the incident wording in v1 |
| `province` / `area` | Only if stated officially and not a precise victim, street or farm location, and only if it does not complete a unique combination under §8 |
| `fact_label` | One of the five CrimeWatch labels, for the claim as specified in §9 |
| `popia_status` | `redacted` or `excluded` |
| `history` | Append-only list of corrections and mapping overrides (§9.3) |
| `raw_off_site` | Raw HTML is not committed. If a local fetch cache is needed for DEV, it stays off the public tree and is deleted when the task ends |

### 4.3 Item record (minimum fields)

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `item_id` | Stable hash of `statement_id` + category or `Unmapped` + disambiguator |
| `statement_id` / `source_url` | Parent statement, with the same join URL |
| `category` | Exact official string from the 44 names below, or `Unmapped` |
| `map_rule` | Short coded reason from `daily_map.py` (for example `saps_wording_matches_leaf` or `no_clear_official_category`) |
| `mapping_status` | `clear_match` or `unmapped` only. This is mapping status, not a fact label, and it must not be shown as Proven, Established, or guilt. |
| `map_attribution` | Shown on the page as CrimeWatch’s interpretation (§6) |
| `fact_label` | Incident or publication fact label only, one of the five labels, kept separate from `mapping_status` |
| `is_rollup_only` | Always false for published items. Daily items are not mapped *only* to a roll-up row (see §4.4) |

Counts shown on the page are **counts of statements or of items**, never “crimes today”. A day with no listed statements (observed: 22 Nov 2026 on the official search page) is shown as “no statements listed”, not as “no crime”.

A day’s summary may only describe sourced, non-excluded items. It must not mention unsourced incident detail, and it must not hint at an excluded item (including missing-person appeals and unique-combination cases). An excluded count, if shown at all, is a bare integer only (for example `excluded: 3`), with no description of what was excluded.

**Roll-up names** (they are official `Crime_Category` values in the same workbook, used for quarterly totals). A daily item is not published with only a roll-up name. If the wording matches a leaf, use the leaf. If it does not, use `Unmapped`. The eight roll-ups are:

**Mapping rules:**

- Use the exact official spelling. Do not rename “Malicious damage to property”, “Robbery of cash in transit”, or “Assault with the intent to inflict grievous bodily harm”.
- Map when the SAPS wording clearly matches one leaf. Titles observed on 23–25 Nov 2026 sometimes did (murder, attempted murder, rape, kidnapping, business robbery, illegal possession of firearms, drug-related crime) and sometimes did not (missing-person appeals, weather warnings, awards ceremonies, “damage to essential infrastructure”, copper-cable seizures, “illegal immigrants”, a Hawks retraction). Those non-matches go to `Unmapped` or, for identifying missing-person appeals, are excluded under §8.
- One statement may produce more than one item if it clearly names more than one leaf. Those items stay linked to one statement and are not summed as separate “crimes”.
- An arrest, a search, a seizure, or a court appearance is not itself a recorded-crime count, and **arrested or charged is not convicted** (§9).
- Never sum daily items into a quarterly total, and never display a daily item count next to a quarterly Proven figure in a way that invites addition.

`Unmapped` is a register state, not a 45th official SAPS category and not a sixth fact label.

The mapping is CrimeWatch’s interpretation, produced by `daily_map.py`. It is not SAPS’s quarterly statistical classification of the incident. Who may override it is set in §6.

### 5.3 Bounded steps (v1)

1. Fetch the official SAPS dated search for today and yesterday (Africa/Johannesburg), so late posts and empty days are handled. Starting URL shape, already used on 25 Nov 2026: `https://www.saps.gov.za/newsroom/news_search.php` with `fdate` and `tdate`.
2. Diff canonical URLs against `data/daily/statements.jsonl`. Fetch only new or edited statement pages. Do not recrawl the whole month.
3. Run the POPIA gate. Fail closed. Excluded items are logged as `excluded`, without storing private identifiers or unique-combination fields, and they are not passed into the RFC-005 day summary.
4. Map surviving items to a leaf category or `Unmapped`. Assign “SAPS claim” framing and fact labels (§9). Record `map_rule`.
5. Write only under `data/daily/`. If the same pass prepares an RFC-005 day object, use the same join URL and the same redacted wording, and do not skip RFC-005’s review-before-publish. Build the daily register page behind a feature flag (off by default), on the private draft surface until a public-page GO. 6. Re-use, do not expand, the sweep’s existing social and news checks: they remain leads. A lead may be stored as a cite-and-link pointer, or left on the private draft surface, but it is not counted in the daily category register until a SAPS (or, later and not in v1, original court) source exists.
7. Stay quiet unless the fetch fails, the gate fails, or new excluded-but-sensitive classes need a reviewer. No chatty success reports.

## 8. POPIA

Live CrimeWatch method rule (https://crimewatch.grokforum.site/method/, 20 November 2026 edition): private victims and suspects appear only as totals; no names, addresses, phone numbers or photographs of private people; no street, farm or victim location; public officials only in their public role.

Daily statements make that rule easy to break. Headlines on the official 25 Nov 2026 search page included missing-child appeals and a named murder case. Publishing those headlines as-is would put private victims, children and unconvicted suspects on a public, potentially indexable page.

Owner rule already adopted for RFC-005 (t704u) also applies here, including the exact disclaimer in §6 and the rule that every fact on our page needs a reference.

**Redaction and exclusion rules (fail closed):**

1. **Scan** every title, claim, URL query string, day summary and generated page for SA ID numbers, passport numbers, phone numbers, emails, physical addresses, bank or account numbers, number plates, precise locations, private photographs, credentials and keys (popia-review checklist).
2. **Drop** names, ages and other identifiers of private people, including unconvicted suspects and all children. Replace with role-or-total wording (“an adult suspect”, “a child victim”, “three people arrested”) only where that wording is necessary **and** does not complete a unique combination under rule 4.
3. **Do not** connect an anonymous account, a precise place, and an incident into a profile of a private person.
4. **Unique-combination rule:** a combination of date + place + role/age (especially of minors) stays off our page even if SAPS publishes it. “A 14-year-old in [town] on [date]” is identifying and is excluded or further generalised until it is no longer a unique combination. 5. **Missing-person appeals:** exclude from the register and from every day summary (`popia_status: excluded`). Owner decision, 26 Nov 2026, 08:06 SAST.
6. **Public officials:** officials by role only, and only from the official source. Leave out family, home, health and private contact details, and do not use the official’s personal name on our page. Owner decision, 26 Nov 2026, 08:06 SAST.
7. **Photographs and video:** do not copy images of private people from statements, social posts or news.
8. **Storage and retention:** do not commit unredacted statement bodies, victim names or raw HTML to the public repo, wikis, pages, handoffs or shared chats. Temporary fetch caches are deleted when the task ends. Redacted register records are kept until a request via `privacy@grokforum.site`; then they are removed from every surface including the private draft surface, and a private takedown history is kept (decision log, (a)). ## 9. Methodology fit

CrimeWatch follows the MediaWatch closed set of five fact labels (Proven, Established, Alleged, Disputed, Not established) and does not invent a sixth. “SAPS claim” is the default **claim framing** for incident wording in this register, not a sixth fact label. Official SAPS wording and statistics record what was charged, counted or classified, not “what happened”. Official and other figures sit side by side and are never added together (live method page, 20 November 2026 edition).

**Proven means only that the statement was published, never guilt.** Arrested or charged is not convicted, and those statuses must be worded as arrest or charge, never as a Proven offence or a conviction.

Default labels for this product:

| Kind of claim | Incident framing / fact label | Mapping status (separate) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| The incident wording taken from a SAPS statement | Framing: **SAPS claim** | — | Default for every incident row in v1. The incident label is never taken from the mapping. |
| “SAPS published this statement at this URL on this date” | Proven | — | Proves publication only; never guilt; never that the named offence occurred as described |
| “SAPS wording in that statement names this official leaf category” | Remains a **SAPS claim**; not set to Established | `clear_match`, shown as CrimeWatch’s interpretation | A clear wording match is mapping status only. It must not read as Established guilt or as an Established incident. |
| Mapping that is uncertain | Remains a **SAPS claim**; Alleged or Not established if a fact label is needed | `unmapped` | Do not force a leaf. `Unmapped` is mapping status, not a sixth fact label. |
| Arrest, charge, or “to appear in court” | SAPS claim; not Proven as guilt | Mapping status follows the offence wording, if any | Must say arrested or charged, not convicted |
| Social post or news RSS lead | Alleged | Cite-and-link only; not in category totals |
| Official retraction or conflicting official wording (the 25 Nov search list included a Hawks retraction) | Disputed, and the row is updated through the correction path | Keep history |
| Court verdict taken from an original court or judiciary publication | Proven as to the verdict recorded, and not in v1 | Still not a SAPS recorded-crime count; a prior arrest or charge on the same matter is not rewritten as a conviction until this source exists |
| Quarterly SAPS national or provincial totals | Proven, unchanged, quarterly pages only | Daily items never join these totals |

Derived counts (statements per category per day) take the label of their inputs and must be captioned as statement-item counts. They are not Proven crime totals, and they are not Proven guilt.

### 9.3 Correction path (keeps history)

Mapping errors and SAPS edits are corrected without deleting the earlier record.

- If `daily_map.py` or a reviewer finds a mapping error, the item is updated, and `history` appends the previous category, `map_rule`, fact label, the new values, the reason, who made the change, and the timestamp (Africa/Johannesburg).
- If SAPS edits, replaces, or retracts a statement at the same canonical URL, the register is fetched again, the row is updated (label Disputed where official wording now conflicts), and `history` keeps the previous redacted claim and labels. A new URL is a new statement, joined separately, with a link to the earlier one if that is itself official.
- Overrides follow §6: only the owner or the critic reviewer named in the decision log, never a silent bot rewrite of a reviewed row.
- History is not shown in a way that reintroduces redacted or excluded detail. Taken-down rows follow the process in the decision log, (a).
